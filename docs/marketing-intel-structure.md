# Navy SEAL Marketing Intelligence - Context Collection Structure

## 🎯 Purpose
Gather comprehensive context to enable Level 5 agents to create "holy crap, how did you know that" marketing strategies, positioning, and GTM campaigns.

---

## 1. 🏢 COMPANY FOUNDATION

### Basic Information
- **Company Name**
- **Website URL**
- **Industry/Vertical**
- **Business Model** (B2B, B2C, B2B2C, Marketplace, etc.)
- **Company Stage** (Startup, Growth, Scale, Established)
- **Annual Revenue Range**
- **Team Size**
- **Geographic Markets Served**

### Current State
- **Current Positioning Statement** (how they describe themselves now)
- **Primary Products/Services**
- **Pricing Model & Range**
- **Current Marketing Channels** (what's working/not working)
- **Monthly Marketing Spend**
- **Current Conversion Metrics** (visitor→lead→customer)

### The Story
- **Founder Story** (why did they start this company?)
- **Mission/Vision** (what change do they want to create?)
- **Core Values** (what do they stand for?)
- **Unique Advantages** (what do they have that competitors don't?)
- **Company Culture** (how would employees describe working there?)

---

## 2. 👤 IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE (ICP)

### Demographics
- **Job Title/Role** (be specific: "VP of Sales at SaaS companies" not "executives")
- **Years of Experience**
- **Company Size** (their employer)
- **Industry Vertical**
- **Age Range**
- **Income/Budget Authority**
- **Geographic Location**
- **Team Size They Manage**

### Psychographics
- **Personality Type** (analytical, driver, expressive, amiable)
- **Decision-Making Style** (consensus, authoritative, data-driven)
- **Risk Tolerance** (early adopter vs. conservative)
- **Career Aspirations** (where do they want to be in 5 years?)
- **Personal Values** (what matters most to them?)
- **Status Symbols** (what do they display/brag about?)

### Day in the Life
- **Daily Responsibilities** (what actually fills their calendar?)
- **Key Priorities** (top 3 things they're focused on)
- **Success Metrics** (how is their performance measured?)
- **Reporting Structure** (who do they report to/manage?)
- **Tools They Use Daily**
- **Meeting Types** (what meetings are they in all day?)

---

## 3. 💔 VOICE OF CUSTOMER - PROBLEMS

### Surface-Level Problems (what they say)
- **Stated Pain Points** (what they complain about publicly)
- **Budget Constraints**
- **Time Constraints**
- **Resource Limitations**
- **Technical Challenges**

### Deep-Level Problems (what they feel)
- **3 AM Worries** (what keeps them up at night?)
- **Career Fears** (what could derail their success?)
- **Imposter Syndrome Triggers**
- **Political Challenges** (internal company dynamics)
- **Personal Reputation Risks**

### Problem Prioritization
- **Urgent vs. Important Matrix** (which problems need solving NOW?)
- **Cost of Inaction** (what happens if they don't solve this?)
- **Failed Solution Attempts** (what have they tried that didn't work?)
- **Blame Patterns** (who/what do they blame for these problems?)

---

## 4. 💭 VOICE OF CUSTOMER - DESIRES

### Functional Desires (what they need)
- **Measurable Outcomes** (specific results they want)
- **Process Improvements**
- **Efficiency Gains**
- **Risk Reductions**
- **Capability Additions**

### Emotional Desires (what they want to feel)
- **Confidence Boosters** (when do they feel most confident?)
- **Recognition Needs** (how do they want to be seen?)
- **Control/Autonomy Desires**
- **Security/Stability Needs**
- **Innovation/Cutting-Edge Desires**

### Social Desires (how they want to be perceived)
- **Peer Perception** (how do they want colleagues to see them?)
- **Leadership Perception** (how do they want bosses to see them?)
- **Team Perception** (how do they want reports to see them?)
- **Industry Perception** (how do they want to be known in their field?)

### Transformation Story
- **Current Identity** ("I am someone who...")
- **Desired Identity** ("I want to be someone who...")
- **Success Vision** (paint the picture of their ideal future)

---

## 5. 🚫 VOICE OF CUSTOMER - OBJECTIONS

### Surface Objections (what they say)
- **Price Concerns** ("It's too expensive")
- **Time Concerns** ("We don't have time to implement")
- **Authority Concerns** ("I need to check with my boss")
- **Need Concerns** ("We're happy with current solution")

### Hidden Objections (what they really think)
- **Trust Issues** ("How do I know you can deliver?")
- **Change Resistance** ("This will disrupt everything")
- **Political Concerns** ("This might make my team redundant")
- **Past Trauma** ("We tried something similar and it failed")
- **Identity Threats** ("This challenges how I've always done things")

### Objection Patterns
- **Timing of Objections** (when in the process do they arise?)
- **Objection Sources** (who raises which objections?)
- **Real vs. Smokescreen** (which objections are real blockers?)

---

## 6. 🎯 COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

### Direct Competitors
- **Top 3 Competitors** (who do they lose deals to?)
- **Competitor Strengths** (what do competitors do better?)
- **Competitor Weaknesses** (where do competitors fall short?)
- **Competitor Positioning** (how do they message?)
- **Price Comparison**

### Indirect Competition
- **Alternative Solutions** (DIY, hiring, status quo)
- **Budget Competition** (what else competes for this budget?)
- **Attention Competition** (what else demands their focus?)

### Competitive Wounds
- **Lost Deal Stories** (specific examples of losses)
- **Customer Defection Stories** (who left and why?)
- **Feature Envy** (what do they wish they had?)

---

## 7. 🔍 MARKET INTELLIGENCE

### Industry Trends
- **Emerging Trends** (what's changing in their industry?)
- **Declining Practices** (what's becoming obsolete?)
- **Regulatory Changes** (what new rules affect them?)
- **Technology Shifts** (what new tech impacts them?)

### Market Gaps
- **Unmet Needs** (what is no one solving well?)
- **Underserved Segments** (who is being ignored?)
- **Overserved Areas** (where is there too much competition?)

### Category Dynamics
- **Category Leaders** (who defines the space?)
- **Category Expectations** (what's table stakes?)
- **Category Evolution** (where is it heading?)

---

### What's Not Working

- **Wasted Spend Areas**
- **Message Mismatches**
- **Channel Failures**

### Marketing Scars
- **Past Agency Failures**
- **Internal Political Battles**
- **Budget Cuts/Constraints**
- **Team Skill Gaps**

## 9. 🚀 TRANSFORMATION OPPORTUNITY

- **Message Refinements**
- **Channel Optimizations**
- **Offer Improvements**

### Strategic Opportunities

- **Industry "Givens" They Don't Question**

## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR CONTEXT COLLECTION

1. **The 5 Whys**: Always dig deeper - the first answer is rarely the real answer
2. **Emotional Peaks**: Pay attention when energy shifts in conversation
3. **Story Mining**: Specific stories reveal more than general statements
4. **Contradiction Gold**: Gaps between saying and doing reveal truth
5. **Language Patterns**: How they say it matters as much as what they say
6. **Silence Power**: Let them fill awkward silences with truth
7. **Example Prompts**: "Tell me about a time when..." gets better intel

6. PRICING STRATEGY
==================================================
Mayo Clinic employs a tiered pricing structure aligned with service complexity:

- Standard Care Services: Market-rate pricing with 15-20% premium reflecting brand value
- Specialty Programs: Premium pricing 30-40% above market for unique treatments
- Executive Health Program: $5,000-$10,000 per comprehensive assessment
- International Patient Programs: Custom pricing with 25-35% markup for coordination
- Insurance Partnership Rates: Negotiated rates with 10-15% preferred network discounts
- Direct Employer Programs: Volume-based corporate wellness packages starting at $250/employee
- Digital Health Solutions: Subscription model ranging $29-199/month
- Medical Education: Tiered pricing from $500-5,000 for professional development

Competitive analysis shows Mayo commanding 20-30% premium vs regional healthcare providers, justified by outcomes data and brand equity.
